What this concept is testing
  • Can a viewer distinguish instantaneous simulator truth from information actually received over the network?
  • Can they identify an incomplete image, a stale aircraft update and a historical packet route without relying on color alone?
  • Does a focused route plus exceptions communicate better than the complete graph, while preserving access to diagnostic detail?

Implementation prerequisites: real serialized telemetry state, acquisition/receipt timestamps, receive-only storage, original-deadline image reassembly, typed planner/fault events, and measured lifecycle states. Current telemetry packets do not contain the illustrated state fields. This page changes no simulator code, requires no network and uses no external libraries, fonts or assets.

Design basis: the supplied UAV-X rubric, the human-swarm display literature, ROS/Foxglove timestamp semantics and published competition evidence practices. Source ledger: visual-demo-sources.json. Shape, text and line pattern reinforce color; this is not a claim of complete accessibility certification.
